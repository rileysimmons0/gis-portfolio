About Me

I've always been interested in geography and understanding how the places around us work.

That interest led me to study Geography at the University of Minnesota, and I'm currently pursuing a master's degree in Geographic Information Science (GIS) there. I've enjoyed learning how GIS can be used to solve real-world problems and make geographic information more useful.

Through my experience working with local governments, I've especially enjoyed seeing how GIS can support communities, whether that's managing infrastructure, creating maps, or finding better ways to organize and visualize information.

Outside of GIS, I enjoy photography, exploring the outdoors, camping, and traveling. I've always liked discovering new places, which ties back to my interest in geography.
